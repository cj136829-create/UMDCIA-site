Our Mission

Who We Are &
Why We Do This

We are a cohort‑driven community of students, faculty, and industry partners who believe that analytics is more than numbers — it’s a way to understand people, solve problems, and shape smarter business decisions. The CIA program brings together curious, motivated students who learn side‑by‑side through hands‑on projects, competitions, and real client work. Our culture is built on collaboration, mentorship, and a shared commitment to turning data into meaningful insight.

We believe students learn best when they’re challenged, supported, and given opportunities that mirror the real world. That’s why the CIA program emphasizes experiential learning: working with companies, presenting to executives, competing in analytics challenges, and building the technical and strategic skills employers look for. Our goal is simple — to prepare students to enter the workforce as confident, capable analysts who can transform data into impact from day one.
